### 1. Sales Navigator Email Reveal Automation
Sales Navigator's "Get Email" button (for accounts where unlocked emails are available) requires a human-like click-and-wait interaction. The extension automates this: for each profile in the extraction queue, it clicks the email reveal button, waits for the email to appear in the DOM (using MutationObserver), extracts the revealed email, and continues — automating what would take an SDR 5+ hours manually.

### 2. Job Change Signal Detection
Sales Navigator shows "Changed jobs recently" badges on profiles. The extension detects this badge and includes a `job_change_signal` flag in the export — a high-value signal for B2B outreach as job changers are often evaluating new vendors. This converts a visual UI element into an actionable CRM data point.

### 3. Rate-Limit-Compliant Pagination
LinkedIn's Sales Navigator throttles aggressive automated navigation. The extension implements a human-behavioral delay model: 5-15 second randomized delays between profile extractions (not page loads), 30-60 second delays between pages, and an automatic 5-minute pause every 200 profiles — staying well within LinkedIn's rate thresholds while completing large extractions.

### 4. Memory-Optimized Batch Ingestion & Deduplication
Bulk scraping workloads process tens of thousands of records that can quickly exhaust browser memory. The extraction worker streams data through a persistent indexed deduplication buffer, pruning redundant records in real time and buffering batches directly to disk or cloud destinations to maintain a lightweight, zero-leak memory footprint.
