### 1. Velocity-Based Early Detection Algorithm
The extension measures trend velocity — the rate of engagement acceleration, not absolute engagement level. A topic with 1,000 mentions growing at 300% in 2 hours is a more significant emerging trend than a topic with 1 million mentions growing at 5%. This velocity-first approach surfaces trends in their early phase before volume metrics make them obvious.

### 2. Cross-Platform Trend Correlation
A topic appearing on Twitter/X AND rising simultaneously on Reddit AND showing Google search volume increase is a high-confidence emerging trend. The extension correlates signals across platforms — topics with cross-platform presence get a higher confidence score and trigger immediate notification, filtering noise from platform-specific viral content.

### 3. GPT-4o-mini Content Angle Suggestions
When a trend is detected, GPT-4o-mini generates 3-5 content angle ideas tailored to the trend — considering the team's configured industry/niche. For example, a trending tech topic generates content angle suggestions like "beginner's explainer", "industry implications" and "contrarian take" — helping the team immediately identify the most appropriate angle for their audience.

### 4. Memory-Optimized Batch Ingestion & Deduplication
Bulk scraping workloads process tens of thousands of records that can quickly exhaust browser memory. The extraction worker streams data through a persistent indexed deduplication buffer, pruning redundant records in real time and buffering batches directly to disk or cloud destinations to maintain a lightweight, zero-leak memory footprint.
