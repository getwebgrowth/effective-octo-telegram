### 1. Wappalyzer-Based Tech Stack Detection
For each prospect's website, the extension runs Wappalyzer's technology detection fingerprinting engine (injected as a content script) to identify the tech stack — surfacing React, Vue, Angular, Next.js, Node.js, and 1,000+ other technologies. Tech stack data is a critical ICP signal for developer tool sales.

### 2. Configurable ICP Scoring Model
The ICP criteria and scoring weights are fully configurable via a JSON editor in the extension settings. Each criterion (employee count, industry, funding stage, tech stack, geography) has a weight and match condition. Prospects are scored against all criteria and classified into A/B/C tiers — allowing the sales team to focus only on the highest-probability targets.

### 3. ProductHunt & Crunchbase New Company Monitoring
The extension monitors ProductHunt for newly launched products and Crunchbase for recently funded companies — surfacing prospects that are likely actively investing in new software tools. Recently funded startups and newly launched products are high-intent prospects for SaaS and developer tool companies.

### 4. Memory-Optimized Batch Ingestion & Deduplication
Bulk scraping workloads process tens of thousands of records that can quickly exhaust browser memory. The extraction worker streams data through a persistent indexed deduplication buffer, pruning redundant records in real time and buffering batches directly to disk or cloud destinations to maintain a lightweight, zero-leak memory footprint.
